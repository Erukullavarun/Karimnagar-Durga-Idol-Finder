About the Platform

A community directory for Karimnagar

Karimnagar Durga Idol Finder brings every sacred Durga idol, Navratri mandapam, and Bathukamma celebration across town into one centralized place, so devotees, families, and visitors can plan an auspicious route and discover the pandals worth a special visit.

✨

What this site does

Every Sharad Navratri, hundreds of Durga Mata mandapams are erected across Karimnagar colonies, each adorned with distinct alankarams, theme decorations, Chandi Yagnams, and Dandiya nights. Finding them usually means asking around locally.

This live directory organizes all mandapams onto an interactive GPS map with verified photos, area details, and turn-by-turn driving routes. It is designed to be easily managed each year: administrators can add, verify, and update listings directly from the dashboard.

🛡️

Our verification standard

Only listings verified against genuine pandal coordinates and photos are published on the map and directory. Unverified submissions remain in review. A misplaced map pin could send a family to the wrong lane on a festive evening—which is worse than no pin at all.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Karimnagar Durga Idol Finder? ▼

It is a community platform and interactive directory bringing together every Durga Mata mandapam and Navratri celebration across Karimnagar. It helps devotees discover idols, view photos, and navigate effortlessly.

How do I get turn-by-turn driving directions? ▼

Every listing includes a direct GPS driving button. Tapping it opens Google Maps on your phone with instant turn-by-turn GPS navigation straight to the mandapam.

Is listing a Durga mandapam free? ▼

Yes, listing is 100% free. This is a non-profit community initiative dedicated to celebrating and supporting our local festivities across Karimnagar.

How do I submit photos or update details? ▼

Click "+ Add Durga" in the top navigation bar, browse 2 to 3 photos directly from your phone/PC, enter your pandal details, and submit for verification.
